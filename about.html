About AmazeMed

AmazeMed is a dev shop where developers are doctors. We combine clinical expertise with software engineering to build productivity tools that healthcare professionals actually need.

Our unique approach comes from having team members who are both practicing physicians and experienced developers. This dual perspective allows us to understand problems from both sides—the clinical reality and the technical possibilities.

Mission

AmazeMed was founded to bridge the gap between healthcare needs and technology solutions. Too often, healthcare software is built by developers who don't understand clinical workflows, or by clinicians who lack technical depth. We combine both.

Our mission is to build tools that reduce administrative burden, enhance patient care, and integrate seamlessly into existing healthcare systems—all while maintaining the highest standards of clinical safety and technical excellence.

The Physician-Developer Advantage

What makes us different is that our developers are also physicians. This means:

  • We understand clinical workflows because we live them daily
  • We know what problems are worth solving and which ones aren't
  • We can validate solutions in real clinical environments
  • We build with clinical safety as a first principle, not an afterthought
  • We speak both languages—clinical and technical—fluently

Our Approach

Every project starts with understanding the real problem. We don't build technology for its own sake—we build solutions that address genuine clinical needs. Our development approach emphasizes:

  • Clinical First: Every decision prioritizes clinical workflows and patient safety
  • Reliability: We build systems that are deterministic, verifiable, and safe
  • Integration: Tools that work with existing systems, not against them
  • Iteration: Rapid cycles of development and validation in real clinical contexts
  • Evidence-Based: Solutions grounded in clinical evidence and validated workflows

Values

  • Clinical Safety: Patient safety and clinical appropriateness guide every decision
  • Technical Excellence: We build robust, maintainable, and scalable systems
  • Practical Innovation: Technology that solves real problems, not theoretical ones
  • Transparency: Clear communication about capabilities, limitations, and trade-offs
  • Collaboration: We work with healthcare organizations as partners, not vendors
